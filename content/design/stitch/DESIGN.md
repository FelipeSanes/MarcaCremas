## Brand & Style
The design system embodies modern cosmetic editorialism—a sensory, tactile space balancing dermal science with botanical luxury. Designed for discerning beauty consumers who prioritize conscious self-care, ingredient purity, and curated routines.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with soft **Sensory Tactility**:
- Ample negative space allows formulation details and packaging photography to breathe.
- Color applications remain airy, warm, and restorative, evoking botanical extracts, silken balms, and clean skin.
- Typography establishes tension between classic editorial serifs (poised, sophisticated, traditional apothecary prestige) and modern, legible geometric sans-serifs (structured, functional, precise).
- Visual elements embrace soft containment with gentle radii, whisper-light borders, and micro-diffused ambient light.

## Colors
The color palette creates a serene, restorative sanctuary utilizing warm botanical tones and soft petal accents:

- **Primary (`#6B5B95` - Muted Royal Lavender)**: Anchor tone used for primary interaction points, definitive actions, prominent headers, and high-emphasis editorial accents.
- **Secondary (`#E8B4B8` - Soft Dusty Blush)**: Used for subtle highlight badges, organic promotional states, and warm decorative moments. A tint variant (`#F3D2D6`) serves as gentle selection tints and hover fills.
- **Tertiary (`#BCA0DC` - Sheer Petal Lilac)**: Soft focal support, ingredient highlights, and secondary graphic containers. Pair with `#D8C7E8` for subtle wash effects.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` & `#F8F4EF` - Warm Alabaster & Raw Silk)**: The underlying canvas foundation. Avoid pure sterile whites (`#FFFFFF`); use `#FDFBF7` for the main background and `#F8F4EF` for cards, layered sections, and inner wells.
- **Typography & Structural Inks**:
  - Text Primary: `#2A2533` (Deep Velvet Charcoal) ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility against cream backdrops.
  - Text Muted: `#756C7D` (Soft Mauve Slate) for metadata, captions, and secondary notes.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#ECE4DA` (Sheer Linen) at 1px thickness to gently separate surfaces without harsh lines.

## Typography
The system balances two typographic voices to preserve editorial weight while maintaining rapid commerce legibility:

- **Playfair Display**: Assigned to hero headings, product titles, collection intros, and pull quotes. It provides high-contrast, graceful serifs that evoke luxury fragrance and couture packaging.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Assigned to all transactional elements, pricing, navigation, specs, filter chips, and running body text. Its rounded geometry complements Playfair Display’s soft contours while offering optimal clarity across small screens.
- **Uppercase Styling**: Labels (`label-lg`, `label-md`) must always render in uppercase with tracked spacing (`letterSpacing >= 0.08em`) to act as refined anchors for categories and ingredient claims (e.g., "COLD-PRESSED", "VEGAN FORMULATION").

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column responsive fluid grid with deliberate breathing room around product imagery:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid, `margin`: `3rem` (max-width `1440px`), `gutter`: `1.5rem`. E-commerce listings utilize 3 or 4 columns per product card.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `margin`: `2rem`, `gutter`: `1.25rem`. Product listings reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `margin-mobile`: `1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile`: `1rem`. PDP and listings collapse to 1 or 2 columns with horizontal snap-scroll rails for secondary collections.

Spacing scales prioritize generous whitespace: section gaps should use `space-2xl` to mimic printed magazine spreads, allowing each product curation its own spotlight.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids heavy structural drops, relying instead on delicate tonal layering and warm ambient diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: The canvas (`#FDFBF7`) transitions into cards or sections using the secondary neutral surface (`#F8F4EF`) bordered by 1px solid `#ECE4DA`.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift / Product Cards)**: Rest state uses no shadow with a hairline border (`#ECE4DA`). On hover, lift using a warm, deeply diffused glow: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(107, 91, 149, 0.06), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(42, 37, 51, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky ATC, Floating Filters)**: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(107, 91, 149, 0.08), 0px 6px 12px -3px rgba(42, 37, 51, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Cart Drawer, Modals)**: Backed by a warm translucent overlay (`rgba(42, 37, 51, 0.3)` with a `4px` backdrop blur). Surfaces employ a diffuse ambient shadow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(42, 37, 51, 0.14)`.
- **Glassmorphic Tints**: Sticky navigation headers and floating filter pills use `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)` with `12px` backdrop blur and a `1px` bottom border of `#ECE4DA`.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides an organic, tactile silhouette that mirrors cosmetic compacts, serum droplets, and polished stones:
- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Dialogue boxes)**: `0.5rem` (`rounded-base`).
- **Containers & Product Image Wrappers**: `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) up to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) for hero banners and featured editorial blocks.
- **Pills / Full Radius**: Interactive chips, badges, and primary action buttons utilize full-pill treatment (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and gentle ergonomic handling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Bag")**: Full-pill radius (`9999px`), solid primary background (`#6B5B95`), white text (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Typography: `label-lg`. Subtle hover shift to `#594982` with soft lilac shadow glow.
- **Secondary CTA ("Explore Formulation")**: Full-pill radius, background `#F8F4EF`, border 1px solid `#BCA0DC`, text `#6B5B95`. On hover, background shifts to `#F3D2D6` with zero border jump.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, underline offset `6px` with `#6B5B95`, no pill frame. Used for editorial links and secondary navigations.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#F8F4EF` with `rounded-lg` (16px), 1px border of `#ECE4DA`.
- **Image Frame**: Dedicated aspect ratio (4:5) with soft cream inner fill, subtle image zoom (`scale(1.03)`) on container hover.
- **Badges**: Positioned absolute top-left; mini-pill badge with `#F3D2D6` background and `#6B5B95` text (`label-md`).
- **Typography Layout**: Product collection/type in `label-md` (`#756C7D`), title in `headline-sm` (`#2A2533`), followed by price in `price-md` (`#6B5B95`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Off**: Background `#F8F4EF`, border 1px solid `#ECE4DA`, text `#756C7D`, `rounded-full`, padding `8px 16px`.
- **State Selected**: Background `#D8C7E8`, border 1px solid `#6B5B95`, text `#2A2533`, subtle checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#ECE4DA`, text `#2A2533`, `rounded-base` (8px), padding `12px 16px`. Focus state shifts border to `#6B5B95` with a 3px ring of `rgba(188, 160, 220, 0.25)`.
- **Quantity Steppers**: Segmented pill component (`rounded-full`), `#F8F4EF` fill, minus/plus icons in `#6B5B95`, central value in `price-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: 18px box, `rounded-base` (4px), border 1.5px solid `#BCA0DC`. Checked state fills `#6B5B95` with a crisp white tick.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric rings with 3px inner gap, outer border `#6B5B95`, center circle `#6B5B95`.

### Skincare-Specific Components
- **Ingredient Accordion**: Minimalist hairline dividers (`#ECE4DA`), trigger headers in `title-md`, expanding to reveal key actives highlighted with `#E8B4B8` bullet anchors.
- **Sensory Swatch Picker**: Circular swatches (`32px`) displaying formulation textures (balm, serum, mist) wrapped in an active indicator ring (`#6B5B95` with 2px offset).
